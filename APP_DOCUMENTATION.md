### 1.2 Full Description (English)

**Free IPTV Player** is a lightweight, ultra-responsive media player engineered natively for Samsung Smart TVs running Tizen OS. Designed from the ground up for the living room 10-foot TV experience, Free IPTV Player provides seamless navigation, hardware-accelerated 4K/FHD playback, and deep compatibility with standard IPTV playlists.

> **CRITICAL LEGAL NOTICE & DISCLAIMER**  
> **Free IPTV Player is strictly an independent media playback client application.**  
> It does **not** bundle, host, provide, scrape, index, or distribute any streaming channels, live broadcasts, movies, series, or video content of any kind. Users must provide their own legally obtained playlist (M3U URL or Xtream Codes API credentials) from their authorized IPTV provider.

#### Key Features:

* **Dual Provider Ingestion:**
  * **Xtream Codes API:** Direct integration with Xtream Codes servers. Log in securely with Server URL, Username, and Password to auto-populate Live TV, VOD Movies, and TV Series.
  * **M3U & M3U8 Playlists:** Instant loading and high-speed parsing of standard M3U/M3U8 URLs and local playlist files.

* **Native Samsung Hardware Acceleration (AVPlay):**
  * Direct integration with Samsung Tizen `webapis.avplay` for hardware-decoded playback.
  * Supports H.264, HEVC / H.265, MPEG-4, TS, MP4, MKV, and HLS adaptive bitrate streaming.
  * Smooth 60fps UI overlays with true transparent hardware video punch-through.

* **Smart Home Hub & Continue Watching:**
  * Clean dashboard with Quick Access cards to Live TV, Movies, Series, Favorites, and Playlists.
  * **One-Click Continue Watching:** Directly resume movies from your exact saved second, or launch the latest watched episode of any TV series without having to navigate menus.

* **Interactive TV Series & In-Player Episode Switcher:**
  * Clean season selection dropdown and categorized episode lists.
  * Progress bars showing watched percentage for partially viewed episodes.
  * **In-Player Episode List:** Change episodes or switch between seasons without stopping or exiting your current video playback.
  * Automatic sequential playback: seamlessly auto-plays the next episode in the series.

* **VOD Movies with Rich Metadata:**
  * Categorized poster grids with genre filtering and instant rating badges.
  * Detailed movie information view featuring title, duration, release year, cast, and synopsis.
  * Auto-resume dialog to easily pick up where you left off.

* **Live TV & EPG (Electronic Program Guide):**
  * Instant category-based channel browsing.
  * Smooth channel zapping with Up/Down buttons while playing live television.
  * Multi-audio track and subtitle selector directly from the player interface.

* **Global & Category Search:**
  * Powerful unified search querying Live TV channels, Movies, and Series concurrently.
  * Category-level search with on-screen TV keyboard support.
  * Quick-jump navigation: press Up from the top row of any grid to instantly focus the search bar.

* **Universal Favorites & Watch History:**
  * Save your favorite channels, movies, and series across multiple playlists.
  * Complete, timestamped watch history with resume points stored securely on your TV.

* **Configurable Offline Content Caching:**
  * Intelligent multi-tier caching (IndexedDB + localStorage) stores categories and catalogs locally.
  * Fast, instantaneous app launch even on slow internet connections.
  * User-adjustable cache refresh interval (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days, or Manual Only).

* **Complete Arabic & RTL Localization:**
  * Full right-to-left layout mirroring and Arabic interface translation.
  * Mirrored remote D-pad controls for natural navigation in Arabic mode.

* **Zero Tracking & Maximum Privacy:**
  * No user accounts required.
  * No external servers, no cloud tracking, no analytics, and zero advertisements.
  * All playlist URLs, credentials, and playback histories remain strictly on your Samsung TV.

## 2. Technical Architecture & How It Works

### 2.1 Technology Stack
* **Language & Syntax:** Pure Vanilla JavaScript (strict ECMAScript 5 / ES6 compatible with Tizen TV Chromium 69 runtime), HTML5, CSS3. Zero heavy frameworks (no React, Angular, Vue, or Webpack dependencies).
* **Package Format:** Tizen Web Widget (`.wgt`), signed with Samsung Author and Distributor certificate profiles.
* **Storage Engine:**
  * **IndexedDB:** Handles high-volume multi-megabyte channel arrays, movie listings, and series caches with asynchronous cursor reads.
  * **localStorage (fiptv_ prefix):** High-speed persistence for active playlist metadata, user preferences, watch history, playback timestamps, and favorites.
  * **In-Memory Cache:** Instantaneous synchronous lookup during active remote navigation.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Does the app include any TV channels or movies?
**A:** No. Free IPTV Player is an empty media player client. You must input your own M3U URL or Xtream Codes login credentials provided by your legal service provider.

### Q: How do I add my playlist?
**A:** When you first open the app, click **"Add Playlist"**. Choose between:
1. **Xtream Codes API:** Enter your Server URL (e.g. `http://example.com:8080`), Username, and Password.
2. **M3U URL:** Enter the full web address (`http://` or `https://`) of your M3U playlist.

### Q: Can I save my favorite channels and movies?
**A:** Yes. Open any movie or series details page and select **"Add to Favorite"**. For Live TV channels, select the favorite star icon. All favorites appear in the dedicated **Favorites** tab on the Home screen.

### Q: How does Continue Watching work?
**A:** Whenever you watch a movie or series episode for more than 5 seconds, your progress is automatically saved to the TV. On the Home screen, the **Continue Watching** section displays your recently watched media. Clicking any card instantly launches playback at your exact saved position.

### Q: Does upgrading the app erase my playlists?
**A:** No. Free IPTV Player updates in-place. Your playlists, credentials, favorites, and watch history are stored in persistent TV storage and are preserved across application updates.
